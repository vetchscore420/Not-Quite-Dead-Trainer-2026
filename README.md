## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Not Quite Dead: Open World Survival**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for scavenging, crafting, exploration, combat, radiation zones, and vehicle use. It is designed around the game's current survival systems and helps players reduce repetitive resource management while preserving the open-world gameplay loop.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed survival experience, faster access to crafting and upgrades, easier exploration of dangerous areas, or additional convenience during solo and private co-op play.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents normal damage from zombies, bandits, environmental hazards, and other threats.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Allows extended running, exploration, and emergency escapes without normal stamina depletion.
- **No Hunger** — Stops hunger from reducing survival effectiveness during long expeditions.
- **Temperature Protection** — Reduces or prevents penalties related to temperature and heat-source mechanics.
- **Radiation Protection** — Protects the player while exploring radiation zones and contaminated locations.
- **Unlimited Resources** — Supports gathering, crafting, cooking, processing, and base preparation by preventing selected resource quantities from decreasing.
- **Easy Crafting** — Reduces the material burden of crafting equipment and preparing survival supplies.
- **Inventory Assistance** — Makes it easier to manage weapons, ammunition, food, medical supplies, crafting materials, and rare survival gear.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents ammunition from being consumed during firearm combat.
- **No Weapon Durability Loss** — Keeps weapons from losing durability during combat and reduces the need for repairs.
- **Enhanced Weapon Upgrades** — Assists with the game's persistent weapon upgrade system, including upgrade levels from U1 through U6.

### 🌍 World

- **Vehicle Assistance** — Helps preserve vehicle fuel and durability while using cars, boats, and helicopters for travel and cargo transport.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options before beginning a long expedition. Unlimited stamina is useful for crossing the island, escaping infected enemies, and reaching abandoned towns, industrial areas, bunkers, and other high-risk locations. Vehicle assistance is recommended when using cars, boats, or helicopters for long-distance travel.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate resource and inventory assistance before collecting large quantities of weapons, ammunition, food, medical supplies, crafting materials, or rare gear. Store valuable loot at the base or in vehicle storage, and disable quantity-related options before trading or completing progression objectives if you want the normal economy and item flow.

### World & Environment

Radiation protection is useful when entering contaminated areas that require a Gas Mask. Resource assistance can simplify base construction, cooking, processing, and equipment preparation. Vehicle options help with fuel, repairs, cargo transport, and expeditions into remote parts of the island.

### Combat & Weapons

Use God Mode, unlimited ammunition, and weapon durability assistance when clearing infected areas, fighting bandits, completing infestations, or exploring bunkers. Weapon upgrade assistance supports the game's persistent U1 to U6 progression. Disable combat options for a more conventional survival challenge.
